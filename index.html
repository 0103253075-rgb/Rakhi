— VI —

One last thing.

Dear Didi,

I could have just sent you a “Happy Rakhi ❤️” message like I usually do.

But you deserve more than a message that disappears into a chat.

So I made you this little corner of the internet.

Not because I needed a reason to tell you that I love you, but because sometimes the things that matter most deserve to be said properly.

Thank you for being my sister.

Thank you for all the little moments, the conversations, the laughs, the arguments, the advice, and all the memories that I probably don’t appreciate enough while they’re happening.

I don’t know exactly where life is going to take either of us.

But wherever we end up, I hope you always know that you’ll have a brother cheering for you, annoying you, worrying about you, and standing beside you.

Always.

Happy Raksha Bandhan, Didi.
